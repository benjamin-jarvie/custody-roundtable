Custody Roundtable

An async, public, expert-sourced process to build a decision tree for Bitcoin self-custody, built from first principles rather than a threat-model list. One question a week, compiled into branches, not forced consensus. Full process in design.md.

How this works

Tap a node to see its question, any video answers from approved contributors (transcribed to text, original kept for promotion), and the live comment thread underneath. Nothing forces agreement. Where experts disagree, the tree branches instead of collapsing to one answer.

Answers are Nostr comments signed with your own key, from a computer or a phone. No account and no sign-up. New to that, or having trouble? Read how to comment. No question has a deadline, so there is no rush.
