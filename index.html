Custody Roundtable

A public decision tree for Bitcoin self-custody, built one question at a time from the answers of people who design and use these setups. Where answers disagree, the tree branches.

How do I know I am the only one who knows this secret, while minimizing dependency on any one piece of hardware or software? Every node follows from this

How this works

One new question opens each week. No question has a deadline, so every published node keeps taking answers for as long as the project runs. A reply months from now is still worth having.

Answers are Nostr comments signed with your own key, from a computer or a phone. There is no account and no sign-up, and nobody can edit or delete what sits under your name, including us. New to that, or having trouble? Read how to comment.

Where answers disagree the tree branches, and each branch carries the name of whoever argued for it. Nothing is forced into consensus. Full process in design.md, node status in BACKLOG.md.
